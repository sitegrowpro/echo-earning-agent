# 🎙 ECHOES IN THE DARK — Voice Script (all 34 lines)

Every spoken word in the game, in story order, with the situation and acting
direction for each line. Phone texts, notes, and objectives are NOT voiced
(they're read on screen — same as Fears to Fathom).

**Cast needed (minimum 4 people):** Jamie (lead, any gender — they're 17),
Daniel/Intruder (same man — 1 male voice), Dot + Dana + 911 + News (can all be
1–2 women), John + Charlie + Cop (can be 1–2 men).

**Recording guide (keep it simple):**
- Any phone in a quiet room (closet full of clothes = perfect studio). No
  music/TV in the background.
- Hold the phone 15–20 cm from your mouth. Record each line as its own file.
- Name files EXACTLY as the ID under each line (e.g. `dot_03.mp3`). MP3 or
  WAV, either is fine.
- Clap once before each take so takes are easy to find. Do 2–3 takes of scary
  lines and send them all — I'll pick the best.
- Whispers: whisper for real, close to the mic. Don't "stage whisper" (loud
  breathy voice) — real quiet whisper, we'll turn it up in the game.
- When done, send me the files and I'll wire every line into its exact scene.

## Chapter 1 — FreshMart (the errand, ~9 PM)

### DOT — warm 50s cashier, seen everything, teasing but kind

**Situation:** Jamie walks up to the register with only some of the groceries.
Dot leans on the counter, amused.

> `dot_01` — friendly, chatty, drops the "bread guy" warning like gossip:
> "Hey, hon. That basket looks... partial. Dairy case is in the back — and if
> you see the bread guy? Tall. Suit. Doesn't blink. Anyway!"

**Situation:** All 6 groceries on the belt. Dot scans, glances at the storm
outside. The store feels too bright and too empty.

> `dot_02` — total first, then quieter, almost worried:
> "...and that's $47.83. ...Rough night out there, huh? Radio says the storm's
> got a name now. They only name the mean ones."

**Situation (only if Jamie asks about the tall man):** Dot laughs it off...
a little too fast. Winks.

> `dot_03` — breezy cover-up, wink you can HEAR:
> "Hon, it's FreshMart at 9 PM. Everybody here looks like a cryptid. That'll
> be $47.83."

**Situation:** Jamie pays and heads for the door. Dot calls after them.

> `dot_04` — warm goodbye with a real warning underneath:
> "Walk safe, hon! And if a tall fella offers you a ride — you run."

**Situation (only if Jamie talks to Dot again after paying):**

> `dot_05` — quick, kind, already helping the next nobody in line:
> "Have a good night, hon! Careful out there — storm's turning mean."

**Situation (only if Jamie tries to leave without paying):** One sharp bark
from behind, off-screen.

> `dot_06` — loud, stern, freezing:
> "HON."

### JAMIE — 17, housesitter, nervous jokes when scared (the player)

**Situation:** Reply options at the register. Light, polite, a little rushed.

> `jamie_01` — quick grateful smile:
> "Thanks, Dot."

> `jamie_02` — trying to sound casual, not quite managing it:
> "Seen a tall man hanging around?"

### JOHN & CHARLIE — two strange men by the dairy case (easter egg scene)

**Situation:** Charlie (nervous, fidgety) nods toward his friend when Jamie
approaches. John (black suit, utterly still) hasn't blinked once.

> `charlie_01` — jittery, deflecting:
> "Ask John. He knows things. ...Mostly about dogs."

> `john_01` — very quiet, very calm, the store hum seems to dip:
> "...Yes?"

**Situation:** Jamie asks about the suit. Charlie suddenly grabs John's sleeve,
panicking about his lost dog.

> `jamie_03` — joking to break the tension:
> "Nice suit. Big night?"

> `charlie_02` — escalating panic, grabbing John's sleeve:
> "John. JOHN. Where is my dog, John?"

> `jamie_04` — backing off mentally, concerned:
> "...Everything okay, guys?"

**Situation:** A long dead pause. Fluorescents buzz. John stares through Jamie.

> `john_02` — flat certainty, like reading a tombstone:
> "Yeah. ...I'm thinking he's back."

> `jamie_05` — nervous laugh, defusing:
> "Back from... where? The dairy aisle?"

**Situation:** Charlie realizes they've said too much. Both men stare at Jamie
one second too long, then walk out into the rain without buying anything.

> `charlie_03` — awkward, rushed, already walking away:
> "Sorry. Wrong John."

## Chapter 2 — Couch, lasagna, TV news (~9 PM)

### TV NEWS ANCHOR — professional broadcast voice, growing unease

**Situation:** Jamie turns the TV on. Cold-open mid-broadcast.

> `news_00` — classic anchor cadence, urgent:
> "...LOCAL NEWS AT NINE. Our top story tonight: Hollow Creek police—"

**Situation:** Jamie sits through the broadcast. Each segment is ~45 seconds
apart. The anchor gets less "professional" each time.

> `news_01` — standard warning read:
> "...police are asking Hollow Creek residents to lock their doors tonight..."

> `news_02` — slower, rattled:
> "...five separate calls about a tall figure standing in yards, watching
> homes..."

> `news_03` — breaks off mid-word into STATIC, then one wrong sentence:
> "...officials say he leaves when approached. He has never— [STATIC] —he is
> never gone..."

**Situation:** The broadcast dies. Static. The house feels colder.

> `news_04` — cheerful signoff that never finishes:
> "...we'll be right back after—"

## Chapter 3 — KNOCK KNOCK (10:05 PM — the centerpiece scene)

### DANIEL — 30s–40s male, friendly-mask over something wrong (AI-voiced now; your family replaces these)

**Situation:** Three slow knocks at the front door, 10 PM, storm outside.
A man's voice through the wood. Friendly. Reasonable. Wrong.

> `daniel_01` — casual, "silly me" energy, but TOO smooth:
> "...hey. Hey. I'm Daniel — the Millers' son. Locked myself out like an
> idiot. Can you let me in? It'll just take a second."

**Situation (only if Jamie says "the Millers don't HAVE a son"):** The mask
slips. He says Jamie's name. Jamie never told him their name.

> `jamie_06` — firm, door-chain voice, scared underneath:
> "The Millers don't HAVE a son. Leave."

> `daniel_02` — hurt act cracking into a command:
> "...Dana always forgets me. Let me IN, Jamie."

**Situation (only if Jamie asks how he knows their name):** A single heavy
knock. Then almost gentle. That's worse.

> `jamie_07` — small, realizing voice:
> "...How do you know my name is Jamie?"

> `daniel_03` — intimate, like a secret between friends:
> "Dana talks about you all the time. Her favorite housesitter... Jamie."

**Situation (only if Jamie stays silent):** Long silence. Then very quietly —
he's smiling, you can tell.

> `daniel_04` — soft, patient, a promise:
> "...okay. Okay. I'll come back later, then, Jamie."

**Situation (only if Jamie texts Dana mid-scene):** Knocks again, confused that
Jamie went quiet.

> `daniel_05` — puzzled, closer to the door:
> "...hello? You still there?"

### THE INTRUDER WHISPERS (same actor as Daniel — this IS him)

**Situation:** Jamie looks through the peephole. 2.5 seconds of silence. Then
this, from RIGHT outside the door:

> `whisp_01` — true whisper, amused, inches away:
> "...I can see your little shadow under the door, Jamie."

## Chapter 6 — 911 (the finale)

### 911 OPERATOR — calm professional woman (AI-voiced now; your family replaces these)

**Situation:** Jamie calls 911 from a hiding spot. Whisper the situation —
the operator keeps them alive with procedure.

> `op_02` — standard call, steady and firm (plays in most runs):
> "Stay on the line. Officers are en route. Hide somewhere with a LOCK — and
> stay QUIET."

**Situation (only if Jamie warned Dana back in Chapter 3):** The operator
already knows the address. Someone called ahead. (Reward for smart play.)

> `op_01` — faster, already-moving tone:
> "Hollow Creek? We already have a car on your street — someone called ahead.
> Stay QUIET."

### POLICE OFFICER — commanding, then relieved (ending C rescue)

**Situation:** Two minutes after the call: sirens, red-blue through the
windows, boots in the hallway, a flashlight beam under the bed finding Jamie's
face.

> `cop_01` — entry shout, then a beat, then warm relief:
> "POLICE! SHOW ME YOUR HANDS— ...Clear! Kid? KID, YOU'RE SAFE NOW."
