    “Home Alone” — a playable night you will not forget.

    How to play

    You are Alex, 15, home alone for one night while your parents work late. Do your chores. Heat your dinner. Answer your texts. Then survive what knocks.

    • WASD move · Mouse look · SHIFT sprint (loud!) · C crouch (quiet)
    • E interact / hold E for long tasks · F flashlight · TAB phone
    • Running, doors and beeps make noise. When he is inside, noise gets you found.
    • You can hide under the bed and in closets. Turn the flashlight OFF when hiding.
    • There are 4 endings. Your dialogue choices and noise matter. ~60 minutes.

    🖥 Desktop + mouse + headphones recommended. Click the game to lock the mouse.
